
SparrowTV Opportunity Brief

# 1. Executive Summary

SparrowTV is poised to disrupt the $80B+ U.S. television advertising market by unlocking under-monetized linear and streaming ad inventory. With over 88% of linear TV ads currently going unsold or poorly monetized, SparrowTV’s technology stack — including dynamic ad insertion (DAI), automatic content recognition (ACR), and planning tools — provides a scalable way to activate this dormant value. The opportunity lies in converting waste into monetized impressions with precision targeting and better yield optimization. This document outlines how SparrowTV can build, grow, and dominate in this evolving landscape.

# 2. The Problem with Linear TV Ad Monetization

Traditional TV ad models rely on broad demographic targeting, leading to massive inefficiencies. Channels sell most inventory in upfronts and scatter markets, and often run promos or PSAs in unsold inventory, leaving billions in potential revenue on the table. Media buyers seek data-driven, measurable campaigns—but linear channels can’t meet those expectations without serious tech upgrades. Advertisers want targeting. Viewers want relevance. Broadcasters want better yield. SparrowTV bridges this gap.

# 3. Technology as the Catalyst

- SparrowTV’s tech platform enables:
    
- Dynamic Ad Insertion across MVPDs, FAST, and linear broadcast
    
- Real-time targeting using ACR and household-level data
    
- Inventory packaging tools for media sales teams
    
- Campaign planning tools for agencies and buyers
    

Together, these tools enable better CPMs, fewer wasted impressions, and new inventory access at scale.

# 4. Revenue-Generating Verticals

These are the high-potential revenue channels SparrowTV can pursue:

- FAST platforms: Monetize mid-day and promo inventory
    
- Local broadcasters: Turn linear into CTV-like ad delivery
    
- MVPDs: Add new ad tech layer across legacy delivery
    
- SMB Self-Serve: Launch geo-targeted campaigns for local service providers
    
- Franchises: Bundle regional buys across national brands (e.g. Subway, Planet Fitness)
    
- Direct-to-Consumer brands: Data-rich inventory with measurable ROI
    
- Political/Advocacy: High intent, localized reach, major budgets every cycle

Potential Sales and marketing Tool

Appendix A

PotentialTool Name: CTV Revenue Recovery Audit

Purpose: To identify and quantify the amount of ad revenue broadcasters, FAST platforms, or MVPDs are leaving on the table due to underutilized or non-addressable inventory—and show them how SparrowTV can recover it.

Recovery Audit Tool – Key Features

1. Supabase Database Schemas

- Total ad inventory (avg. mins/hour)
    
- % of ad inventory currently addressable
    
- CPM by daypart (peak vs. low)
    
- Monthly ad impressions
    
- Vertical or channel type (local, FAST, national, etc.)
    

2. Output Dashboard (Automated Analysis)

After submission, the user receives:

- Addressability Gap Index (e.g., “You’re only monetizing 14% of potential”)
    
- Revenue Recovery Potential (e.g., “You’re leaving ~$3.2M/year in addressable inventory on the table”)
    
- Estimated CPM Lift
    
- Opportunity Segments (Mid-day promos, PSA blocks, non-targeted runs)
    
- Next Steps CTA: Book strategy call / get a custom roadmap


Panel A (Inputs):

- Intuitive slider-based UI for metrics
    
- Dropdown for type of distributor/platform
    
- Pre-filled CPM averages or upload actuals
    

Panel B (Results):

- Visual “Revenue Recovery Meter”
    
- Addressability percentage vs. ideal target
    
- Revenue potential displayed in dollars
    
- “Download Full Audit” + “Book a Call” CTA buttons
